# Grammar Progression

## Curriculum Inventory

Cover the following across the Foundations and RE1 course sequence. Do not force every feature into every Unit.

### Sentence components

- `subject` — 主语
- `predicate` — 谓语
- `object` — 宾语
- `attributive` — 定语
- `adverbial` — 状语
- `complement` — 补语；include subject/object complements when relevant

### Sentence types

- `simple_sentence` — 简单句
- `compound_sentence` — 并列句
- `complex_sentence` — 主从复合句
- `compound_complex_sentence` — 并列复合句

### Clause types

- relative/adjective clause — 定语从句
- object clause — 宾语从句
- subject clause — 主语从句
- predicative clause — 表语从句
- appositive clause — 同位语从句
- adverbial clauses of time, reason, condition, concession, purpose, result, comparison, and place

### Confirmed eight tenses

1. `simple_present` — 一般现在时
2. `simple_past` — 一般过去时
3. `simple_future` — 一般将来时
4. `present_continuous` — 现在进行时
5. `past_continuous` — 过去进行时
6. `present_perfect` — 现在完成时
7. `past_perfect` — 过去完成时
8. `future_in_the_past` — 过去将来时

## Coverage Stages

Track each feature across Units with one of:

- `encounter`
- `notice`
- `controlled_practice`
- `guided_use`
- `independent_use`
- `review`

The longitudinal coverage matrix, not one Unit, proves curriculum coverage. A Unit target must have either:

- a verified source sentence showing the feature; or
- an explicit spiral-review rationale plus a teacher-created model based on already taught content.

## Foundations and RE1

Both levels may encounter the full inventory. Change the depth and independence:

- Foundations: explicit chunking and component labels, shorter examples, more controlled practice, visual/form support, guided sentence building.
- RE1: longer source sentences, clause-boundary analysis, sentence combination, tense contrast, error correction, guided and independent production.

Do not equate Foundations with a younger cognitive level.

## Sentence Analysis Versus Practice

A source sentence may contain several features, and the teacher annotation may identify them all. Each student practice item must still target one primary mechanic unless the objective is explicitly sentence synthesis.

For every practice task:

1. identify the single primary target;
2. provide a source example or teacher-created model;
3. check noticing/comprehension;
4. provide controlled practice;
5. guide learners to use the feature;
6. collect individual evidence;
7. revisit it in later homework or review.

Avoid compound pattern labels that require learners to imitate several unrelated mechanics at once.

## Source-Based Selection

Prefer high-value sentences that:

- reveal subject/predicate boundaries or long noun phrases;
- demonstrate a target tense in meaningful contrast;
- contain a clause learners need to identify or produce;
- recur as a useful writing or speaking pattern;
- support the article's meaning, not grammar in isolation.

Do not select a sentence merely because it is the longest.
