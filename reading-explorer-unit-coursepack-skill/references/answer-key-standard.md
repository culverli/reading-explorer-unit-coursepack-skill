# Answer Key Standard

## Role

Create a separate, compact teacher copy keyed exactly to the final Student Practice Book. Do not hide answers inside the Teaching Outline.

## Required answer types

Provide:

- exact answers for multiple choice, matching, word/phrase search, cloze, forms, summary, and supported/not-supported items;
- the whole corrected or rewritten sentence when the student task requires a complete sentence;
- one strong reference translation plus acceptable variation notes where needed;
- model answers and acceptance criteria for short answers, Bloom questions, retell, and writing;
- a concise rubric for the final functional output.

For every open item, distinguish:

1. what must be present;
2. what may vary;
3. what would make the answer unsupported or incomplete.

## Alignment

Use the same exercise numbers, titles, item order, wording, and final revisions as the Practice Book. Re-run alignment checks after any late student-book edit.

Do not answer an earlier draft. Do not silently correct a flawed student prompt only in the key; repair both files.

## Reading and Bloom answers

Ground each answer in the verified article. For critical-thinking items, accept different conclusions when the student uses accurate article details and a logical reason.

Keep sample answers level-appropriate. They should demonstrate the expected depth without becoming essays students are expected to memorize.

## Writing support

For a short report or paragraph, include:

- required length or range;
- required content parts;
- useful Unit language requirement when applicable;
- one model within the stated range;
- a simple scoring scheme, normally totaling 10 points.

A useful default is:

- content: 4;
- organization: 2;
- Unit language: 2;
- accuracy: 2.

Change the weights when the teacher's purpose differs.

## Layout

Use the same green/gold visual family as the Practice Book, but favor compact answer tables and short teacher notes. Keep each exercise band with its answers and avoid splitting one short answer set across pages.
