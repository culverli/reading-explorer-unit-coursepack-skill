# Teacher-Edited Practice Book Authority

## Why this reference exists

The teacher substantially reformatted the first generated Foundations Unit 1A Practice Book and then taught from the revised version for two successful lessons. Future Practice Books must reproduce the teacher's decisions, not the earlier generated formatting.

One translation exercise bar remained 10.5 pt in the edited pilot because of the manual edit history. Treat 12 pt as the forward standard when it fits; reduce only a long bar that would wrap or crowd.

## Measured spacing

- Page: US Letter portrait, 8.5 × 11 inches.
- Margins: 0.62 inches left/right and 0.55 inches top/bottom.
- Header/footer distance: 0.25 inches.
- Ordinary vocabulary, form, and short completion rows: 1.5 line spacing.
- Short-answer questions, critical-thinking questions, correction/combination prompts, translation prompts, Yes/No correction, and their answer lines: 2.0 line spacing.
- Section headings retain 1 pt before and 3 pt after.
- Directions retain 2 pt before and 3 pt after.
- Short-answer questions generally retain 1 pt after; sentence prompts use about 0.5–1.2 pt after according to the adjacent response line.
- Add visible air between the title block, Name/Class, and first section instead of compressing them into one uninterrupted block.

Do not use a fixed line-spacing value for the whole document. Use 1.5 for controlled response and 2.0 for reading/thinking/writing space.

## English and Chinese boundary

The teacher removed Chinese from:

- Name/Class/Date labels;
- section headings;
- exercise-bar titles;
- task directions;
- writing directions and planning labels.

The teacher retained Chinese only as the source language inside Chinese-to-English translation items. Apply that as the default policy. Do not add `| 中文` subtitles or bilingual directions.

Use `Name` and `Class`; omit `Date` unless the teacher asks for it. Use `Practice Book` without the earlier descriptive tagline.

## Functional layout changes

- Header text is `Created by Culver Li`, right aligned, rather than a publisher/unit running title.
- Use `/` to separate word-bank and useful-language entries, not centered dots.
- Place word banks in pale-gold boxes and allow centered wrapping when needed.
- In find-the-word/find-the-phrase tables, leave the answer cell clean and blank; do not print decorative underline strings inside it.
- Give more width to answer columns: the pilot changed the word table to approximately `30 / 324 / 167 pt` and the phrase table to `30 / 283 / 208 pt`.
- Do not require students to rewrite a complete sentence after every word-form blank. The pilot removed that duplicate step; keep word-form items concise and include genuine full-sentence production elsewhere.
- Use one well-sized answer line, not two cramped lines, for ordinary correction and sentence-combining items.
- Keep two lines for Chinese-to-English translation when a full sentence genuinely needs the space.
- Omit a visible `Title` label before the final writing space unless it serves a real task purpose.

## Regression review

Before delivery, compare the rendered Practice Book against these rules and answer:

1. Is nearly all learner-facing interface language English-only?
2. Is Chinese confined to actual translation input or a teacher-approved exception?
3. Are body text, options, directions, and tables normally 11 pt?
4. Are controlled rows 1.5 spaced and production/thinking rows 2.0 spaced?
5. Are answer areas blank, adjacent, and large enough for the demanded response?
6. Have redundant rewrite lines and decorative answer-cell underscores been removed?
7. Does the first page breathe around the title and Name/Class line?
8. Does every page remain readable without shrinking text to recover page count?

Page count is an outcome, not a target. Preserve the teacher's readability decisions even when a different article needs more or fewer pages.
